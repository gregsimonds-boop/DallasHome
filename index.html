Before your project begins

A clearer way to get started.

A useful first call starts with your Dallas location, project scope, and a realistic discussion of timing and budget. The contractor you choose is responsible for assessment, estimates, and the work.

01

Describe your project

Explain what you want to change, whether you own the home, and what matters most in your new kitchen.

02

Confirm the fit

Ask about service coverage for your Dallas address, similar kitchen projects, insurance, applicable licensing, and estimate availability.

03

Review before hiring

Request a written scope, itemized pricing, payment schedule, permit responsibilities, and timeline directly from the contractor before agreeing to work.
